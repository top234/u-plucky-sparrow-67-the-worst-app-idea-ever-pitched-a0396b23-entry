## The pitch

> ShelfLife scans your fridge, writes a personalised eulogy for every yoghurt that expires, and charges you $4.99 a month to read it aloud at a tiny funeral attended by your neighbours, so no dairy ever dies unmourned.

An app that treats expired yoghurt like a departed loved one — because nothing says "disruptive" like a subscription funeral service for dairy.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node, no dependencies) writes the pitch to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), validating first that it is a single non-empty line of 1–240 characters.
